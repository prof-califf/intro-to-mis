What the arrow means

Every arrow you draw connects a foreign key, a column holding pointers, to the primary key it points at, the column that uniquely identifies each row of the other table. That pairing is the entire idea of a relational database, and it is why the sales table can stay small and clean: names, regions, and prices live once, in their own tables, and JOINs bring them together when a question needs them. In Lab 7 you write these same JOINs by hand.
