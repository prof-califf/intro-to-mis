Why bother

What's in it for you

  • Something real to show. Entry-level jobs now ask for more proof of experience. A finished bootcamp gives you work and a certificate to point to in an interview or on your resume.
  • Skills employers want. Employers say more than a third of their entry-level jobs now require AI skills, nearly triple the share six months earlier (NACE, 2026).
  • Exam points this quarter. See below.

Who they're for

Every business major

  • Not just MIS, Business Analytics, Ops, or MSCM. If you are a management, marketing, or other "non-technical" major, these are for you too.
  • Almost every business job runs on data and technology. Marketing analysts, HR people, and managers use these tools every day, and the people who can work with them have an edge.
  • No extra experience needed. The bootcamps pick up where the labs left off, and every lesson is about a business decision, not computer science.

This quarter

Exam points

  • 2 points on the exam of your choice for each bootcamp you finish.
  • To claim them, upload your certificate to the matching assignment in Canvas (for example, "Excel for Business") and name the exam you want the points on.
  • Due December 6, 2026, at 11:59 pm.
  • No pressure after that. The bootcamps stay up for free. Try one over winter break, or come back when a job or internship asks for one.
