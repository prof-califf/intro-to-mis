About the digital textbook

Case-first, hands-on, one client

Most textbooks teach concepts and hope the business follows. This one starts with the business: a real company, a real system, and what actually happened, then teaches the concepts the story needs. Then you do the work yourself, in the labs, on data that behaves like the real thing.

📖
Case-first learning

Domino's, Healthcare.gov, Nike, Starbucks. Every concept arrives inside a real story with real dates.

🔬
One running client

Cascadia Outfitters, a member-owned gear co-op, runs through the Excel, Tableau, and SQL labs, with an anomaly hidden in the data.

✅
Checkpoint-gated labs

Every lab embeds sequential checkpoints; clearing them all generates a verifiable completion certificate for Canvas.

🤖
AI, on purpose

Lab 1 sets a three-light AI policy in Week 1; you use AI as a tutor all quarter, never as a substitute for thinking.

Group-assigned discussion questions for Wednesday, and a bridge into that week's hands-on lab.

The thread that connects every chapter

The Five Components of MIS

HardwareThe machines
+
SoftwareThe instructions
+
DataThe facts
+
NetworksThe connections
+
PeopleThe judgment

Every information system is built from these five components, and every case in this book is mapped onto them. One rule governs the course: a system is only as strong as its weakest component. Learning to locate the break is the core skill. The labs make you practice it on data.
