Things worth knowing
  • Labs are graded by certificate. Clear every checkpoint, write the reflection, and the lab page generates a PDF. Upload that one file to Canvas.
  • Read the chapter before Monday. Monday covers the concepts and a short case; Wednesday is your group presenting the chapter's discussion questions. Both days assume you have read it.
  • Exam 1 is Monday, October 19 and covers Chapters 1 through 4. Exam 2 is Monday, November 16 and covers Chapters 5 through 11. The final is Tuesday, December 8 at 10:30 AM and is cumulative, with the most weight on the last three weeks.
  • Two reference pages sit outside the weekly schedule and are worth reading when you need them: How the Internet Works, and Connected Devices and IoT. Neither has its own lecture.
  • No class Wednesday, November 11 (Veterans Day) and no class or labs Thanksgiving week (November 23–27).
  • Dates and details can shift. Canvas is the authority if anything here disagrees with it.
