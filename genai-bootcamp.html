Optional · Extra credit

GenAI for Business: Use It, Then Check It

About 3 to 4 hours, at your own paceBuilds on Lab 1 →

Lab 1 taught you to read AI output the way an editor reads a draft. This bootcamp puts that habit to work on real jobs. You write a prompt that gets a usable draft, hand an AI the Cascadia sales file and check its numbers, and test the sources it gives you. Then you decide what data is safe to paste, edit a draft into a memo you would sign, and build a prompt you can reuse every month. You use a free AI tool on the Cascadia data you already know.

WhyWhy this is a business skill, not a tech one

Most people at work will not build AI. They will use it, every day, for first drafts of emails, summaries, analysis, and plans. The people who get value from it are not the ones with the cleverest prompts. They are the ones who give it the right facts, know which data must never go into it, and check what comes back before their name goes on it.

That checking is the course's main idea applied to a new tool. Information has value only when it changes a decision and the change is worth the cost. An AI answer that nobody checked can change a decision for the worse, quickly and confidently. You will see that happen on data where you already know the right answers.

Skeptical of AI? This bootcamp is for you too. It does not ask you to like AI. It asks you to know it well enough to catch it being wrong, to say what should never be pasted into it, and to decide when a person should do the job instead. Every workplace needs people who will say "this answer is wrong" or "we should not use AI for this," and those people are only believed if they know how it works. Skeptics who learn AI are the ones who keep everyone else honest.

How analysts actually use it

Not one prompt and a copy-paste. A working session has seven moves. Look at the data before analyzing it. Plan the analysis, and fix the plan. Run it with the method shown. Check the result in a second tool. Narrow it down with follow-ups. Have Claude argue against the conclusion. Then write for the reader and prepare for their questions. Lesson 2 walks through that whole session on the Cascadia data, and the other lessons use the same habits.

Where you would use it

Marketing: first drafts of customer emails, checked against the offer. Finance: the monthly summary from a reusable prompt, with every number checked. Operations: a first look at an export, confirmed in Excel. MIS: testing an AI tool before the company approves it. Business analytics: drafting a query or code faster, then checking every line. Any role: knowing what you are allowed to paste, and saying no when you are not.

Who this is for, and what you get

It is optional, and you do not need any technical background. You learn in 28 short steps. Some ask a question about using AI well; most have you do something in a real AI tool and then check it yourself. Pass every step, write a short memo, and upload your certificate and your work file to the GenAI for Business assignment in Canvas. Each bootcamp you finish adds 2 points to the exam of your choice; name the exam in your Canvas submission. Your progress saves in this browser.

For a resume: Completed a 28-step bootcamp on using generative AI at work: prompting, checking AI analysis against source data, verifying sources, and data safety. The resume page shows where it goes.

Yellow · AI is the subject, the checking is yours

Use AI in every step that asks you to. Do the checking yourself: the true numbers come from Excel or the table, not from another chatbot. Write the final memo and the memo at the end in your own words.

Before you start

Use the free plan of Claude, or any chatbot that accepts file uploads. Free Claude accounts can run code on a file you upload; check that Code execution and file creation is on under Settings > Capabilities (Claude Help Center). Claude is not on WWU's AI Tools List, so use it only for public data. The Cascadia data is made up for teaching and posted on this site, so it is fine.

You also need Excel, for checking numbers, and the cascadia-sales-2025.csv file. AI answers change from day to day, so the steps grade what you check, not what the AI said.

How the steps work

Each step teaches one small idea, then gives you a task. Do the task in your AI tool, then answer here and press Check. If it is right, the next step opens. If not, you get a note on what to fix. Every step has a hint, and after two tries you can see a solution. There is no penalty for wrong tries.

Your progress and what you type here save in this browser, so you can stop and come back. To start over, use the button.
